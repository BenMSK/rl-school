PPO replaces TRPO's KL constraint with a clipped surrogate objective that "removes the incentive for moving r<sub>t</sub> outside of the interval [1−ε, 1+ε]", which makes it safe to perform "multiple epochs of minibatch updates" on the same batch, something standard policy gradients cannot do. <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347">Schulman 2017</a>

@@ 7
The paper opens with a scorecard. Deep Q-learning "fails on many simple problems and is poorly understood"; vanilla policy gradients "have poor data efficiency and robustness"; TRPO "is relatively complicated, and is not compatible with architectures that include noise (such as dropout) or parameter sharing". <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347">Schulman 2017</a> The root issue is the one <a href="#p-trpo">the TRPO review</a> describes: a policy gradient step of the wrong size collapses the policy, and vanilla methods take exactly one gradient step per sample, so they cannot reuse data without risking that collapse. TRPO's fix needs a conjugate-gradient solve every update.
@@ 8
Write the surrogate in terms of the probability ratio r<sub>t</sub>(θ) = π<sub>θ</sub>(a<sub>t</sub>|s<sub>t</sub>) / π<sub>θold</sub>(a<sub>t</sub>|s<sub>t</sub>), so r = 1 at the start of each update. Then take the minimum of the unclipped term and a term whose ratio is clipped to [1−ε, 1+ε]. The minimum makes the objective "a lower bound (i.e., a pessimistic bound) on the unclipped objective": once the ratio has moved beyond the band in the direction the advantage favours, the gradient is zero, but a move that makes things worse is never ignored. <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347">Schulman 2017</a>

Work one term by hand with ε = 0.2, so the band is [0.8, 1.2]. Â = +2, r = 1.5: unclipped 3.0, clipped 1.2 × 2 = 2.4, min = 2.4, flat in r, so no further push. Â = +2, r = 0.6: unclipped 1.2, clipped 1.6, min = 1.2, gradient active, pulling r back up. Â = −2, r = 1.5: unclipped −3.0, clipped −2.4, min = −3.0, gradient active, pushing the probability down. The objective is flat only when the update would move r further in the direction the advantage already favours (RL School arithmetic from eq. 7). <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347">Schulman 2017</a>

@@ 31
PPO became the default optimiser for policies at every scale. OpenAI Five was "trained using Proximal Policy Optimization" with a 4096-unit LSTM, for 180 days spread over 10 months, and beat the Dota 2 world champions OG on 13 April 2019. <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.06680">Berner 2019</a> OpenAI's dexterous hand (2018) used PPO with the same clip, 0.2. <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.00177">OpenAI 2018</a> InstructGPT (2022) "fine-tuned the SFT model on our environment using PPO" with a per-token KL penalty, and its 1.3B model was preferred to 175B GPT-3. <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155">Ouyang 2022</a> DeepSeek-R1's GRPO (2025) keeps PPO's clipped objective and drops the critic for a group-mean baseline; DAPO (2025) tunes the clip asymmetrically. <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948">DeepSeek-AI 2025</a> <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14476">Yu 2025</a>
@@ 32
Clipping is not a trust region: it removes the incentive to leave the band but does not bound the KL, which is why implementations add early stopping on an approximate KL (Spinning Up's target 0.01). <a class="src" href="https://spinningup.openai.com/en/latest/algorithms/ppo.html">Achiam 2018</a> Much of the performance lives in code, not equations: the reference implementation has 37 details absent from the paper, including Adam ε = 10<sup>−5</sup>, global gradient-norm clipping at 0.5, and value-loss clipping, for which later studies "find no evidence" of benefit. <a class="src" href="https://iclr-blog-track.github.io/2022/03/25/ppo-implementation-details/">Huang 2022</a> Seeds and network sizes swing results: PPO on Hopper scored 61 ± 33 with a (400, 300) network and 2790 ± 62 with (64, 64). <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.06560">Henderson 2018</a> It is on-policy and sample-hungry; at one million steps on HalfCheetah, TD3 reported 9637 against PPO's 1795. <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.09477">Fujimoto 2018</a> And ACER still had slightly better final Atari scores (28 wins to 19). <a class="src" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347">Schulman 2017</a>
